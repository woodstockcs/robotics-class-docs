# Sumo Wrestling Challenge

Build autonomous robots that compete in sumo wrestling matches. This advanced project challenges you to create robust, competitive robots that can sense, strategize, and dominate the ring.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this project, you will be able to:
- Design robust mechanical systems
- Implement advanced sensor strategies
- Create competitive AI behaviors
- Optimize robot performance through iteration

---

## Competition Rules

Sumo robots must:
- **Stay in bounds**: Avoid falling out of the ring
- **Push opponents**: Force other robots out of the ring
- **Detect edges**: Sense the boundary of the arena
- **Find targets**: Locate and pursue opponents
