# Mechanical Systems

Explore advanced mechanical concepts through lift mechanisms. Learn how gears, levers, and linkages can multiply force and create complex motions in robotic systems.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this unit, you will be able to:
- Design mechanical advantage systems
- Build linear, scissor, and telescoping lifts
- Calculate gear ratios and mechanical advantage
- Integrate mechanical systems with electronic control
