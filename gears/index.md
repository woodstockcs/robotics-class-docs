# Gears & Power Transmission

Understand how gears transform speed, torque, and direction in mechanical systems. Master the mathematical relationships that govern gear trains and apply them to robot design.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this unit, you will be able to:
- Calculate gear ratios and mechanical advantage
- Design gear trains for specific applications
- Understand speed vs. torque trade-offs
- Apply gear systems to robot mobility and manipulation

---

## Gear Fundamentals

Gears are fundamental to most robotic systems, allowing you to:
- **Increase torque** for heavy lifting or pushing
- **Increase speed** for rapid movement
- **Change direction** of rotation
- **Reduce motor strain** through mechanical advantage
