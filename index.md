## Course Overview

This robotics course is designed to give students practical experience with:
- **Programming**: Learn to control robots through code
- **Electronics**: Understand sensors, motors, and circuits  
- **Mechanical Design**: Build and modify robot mechanisms
- **Problem Solving**: Tackle real-world engineering challenges

### Learning Path

Our curriculum follows a progressive structure:

1. **Fundamentals** - Basic concepts and tools
2. **Introductory Projects** - Simple robots and mechanisms
3. **Challenge Projects** - More complex competitions and builds

## Course Units

The course is organized into the following units, designed to build skills progressively:

### 🔧 Fundamentals
- [Electronics Basics](electronics) - Circuits, sensors, and components
- [Programming Foundations](programming) - Control structures and logic

### 🤖 Introductory Projects  
- [LED Programming](leds) - Learn basic control with colorful displays
- [Roomba Modifications](roomba) - Understand robot movement and sensors

### 🏆 Challenge Projects
- [Freeze Tag](freeze) - Multi-robot coordination and strategy
- [Sumo Wrestling](sumo) - Autonomous competition robot
- [Mechanical Systems](lifts) - Advanced mechanisms and gears

### 🎲 Enrichment Activities
- [Rubik's Cube Solving](cubing) - Pattern recognition and algorithms

## Assessment

Each unit includes:
- **Hands-on Projects** (70%) - Build and program working robots
- **Documentation** (20%) - Record your process and learning
- **Participation** (10%) - Collaboration and problem-solving
